# Data quality pass before family rollout

Found while building and checking the river view on the operator's archive.
Nothing here has been changed in the data; this issue is the record.

This page is public, so it names no one: no names, no record identifiers, no
places. The operator holds the details privately.

## Names

- Two records hold an archivist's note in the given-name field (a sentence, in
  Polish, saying the person also appears under that name in the documents)
  instead of a given name.

## Families

- One family: 14 children whose recorded order disagrees with their birth
  dates in 37 pairs; four children carry surnames foreign to the family; one
  child appears twice.
- One union joins two people of the same surname who both have male given
  names: most likely two relatives entered as a couple.
- One person is drawn on both sides of the centre of the river, which looks
  like a duplicate union with the same partner.

## Places (123)

- 96 have no region.
- Some hold free text in the region field.
- One city is recorded twice, under two spellings (with and without a
  diacritic).
- Three entries are not places.
