## 11. Uta na ChatGPT, Claude to IA mosusu (MCP)

Misala yango moko efungwami mpo na ba-IA ya libanda na nzela ya MCP, soki mokambi afungoli yango (Bokambi → AFG → MCP).

1. Na lokasa **Compte na ngai**, eteni “Ba-jeton ya kokota mpo na IA”, salá jeton; poná “Ekoki kobongola ebombelo, lokola compte oyo ekoki” soki IA esengeli kokoma. Kopiá yango: emonisamaka kaka mbala moko.
2. Kangisá IA na yo:
   - **Claude Desktop**: tiá connecteur ya lisanga `ax-genealogy.mcpb` (dosie `mcp/ax-genealogy` ya `axgf-cms`) mpe pesá yango adresi ya site mpe jeton.
   - **Claude Code**: `claude mcp add --transport http ax-genealogy https://site-na-yo/mcp --header "Authorization: Bearer <jeton>"`
   - **ChatGPT** (ba-connecteur, mode développeur) to **`claude.ai`** (connecteur ya yo moko): adresi elongo na jeton na kati, `https://site-na-yo/mcp/t/<jeton>`, kozanga authentification.
   - **Cursor** mpe basusu: adresi `https://site-na-yo/mcp` mpe en-tête `Authorization: Bearer <jeton>`.
3. Longolá jeton soki esalelamaka lisusu te, to soki ekoki kozala ebimi libanda.
