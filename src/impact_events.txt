#   kind|from|to|scopes|name in English|name in French|note in English|note in French
#
# kind    era, war, epidemic, migration, event, economy, disaster, invention or
#         discovery.
# from    a year (-3500 is 3500 BC), or a year, month and day (1914-07-28).
# to      empty for a moment; "now" for what goes on; otherwise as `from`.
#         With a day, it is the day after the last one, so that the length
#         reads as the history books give it (1967-06-05 to 1967-06-11:
#         six days).
# scopes  world, a continent (europe, africa, asia, americas, oceania) or a
#         present-day country's ISO 3166 code, comma-separated. "world" is
#         the operator's repertoire, on every timeline; a continent is shown
#         where the family lived on it, a country to those who lived there.
# notes   optional: the micro explanation shown beside a country's landmark.
