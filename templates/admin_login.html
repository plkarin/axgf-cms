Emergency access

The shared --admin-token still opens an administrator session, and it exists for one purpose: getting back in when the .acl file has been lost or every administrator account is locked out. It is not an account — it owns no preferences, and the edit journal records it as emergency-token rather than as a person. Its use is logged as a warning.
